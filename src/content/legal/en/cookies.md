In short: **we do not use tracking cookies**. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics and no social media buttons that follow you around.

## What we store in your browser

- **Your country.** If you choose a country in the header menu, we save it in your browser's local storage (`nb-country`) to show you the right currency and legal forms. It is not a cookie, it is not sent to any server and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Nothing else. The website works just the same if you block local storage: you will simply see prices in Swiss francs.

## Third-party content

- **Booking calendar (Cal.com).** If you turn it on from the free consultation page, your browser connects to Cal.com, which may use its own cookies. That is why it does not load until you accept. If you would rather not use it, you can call us or write to us.
- **Fonts.** They are served from our own server: your browser does not connect to any external service to display them.

## If anything changes

If we ever add a tool that uses cookies, we will ask for your permission first and explain it here.
