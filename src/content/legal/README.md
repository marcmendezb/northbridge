# Legal pages: placeholder templates

Northbridge Advisory is a fictional company. The texts for `privacidad` (privacy), `aviso-legal` (legal notice) and `cookies` are **templates** based on the Swiss FADP (nDSG, in force since 1 September 2023), art. 3(1)(s) of the Swiss Unfair Competition Act (UWG) and, where relevant, the GDPR. **Before any real use they must be reviewed by a lawyer** and completed with real details (company name, registration number, data controllers, hosting and email providers).

This note lives in the repository only; it is not published on the site.
