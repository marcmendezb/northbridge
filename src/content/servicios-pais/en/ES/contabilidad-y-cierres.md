  - q: What do I need to get started?
    a: Your invoices, read access to your bank and the latest annual accounts, if you have them. That is enough to start.

  - q: When will I have my annual accounts?
    a: If your year ends on 31 December, the directors prepare them by 31 March, the shareholders approve them by 30 June and they are filed at the Commercial Registry within the following month.

    a: Yes. We start by bringing it up to date and give you a plan with dates. It is quoted separately, at a fixed price.
