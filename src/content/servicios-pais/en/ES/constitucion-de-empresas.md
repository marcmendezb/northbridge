faq:
  - q: SL or SA?
    a: An SL is simpler and cheaper to run, and suits most SMEs. An SA requires more capital and is usually chosen when there are many shareholders or a listing is planned.
  - q: How much capital do I need?
    a: For an SL, from 1 euro; until the capital reaches 3000 euros, part of the profit must go to a reserve and the partners are liable for the difference. For an SA, 60 000 euros, with at least a quarter paid up.
  - q: How long does it take?
    a: Once all the documents are ready, usually between one and three weeks.
  - q: Can I set up a company if I do not live in Spain?
    a: Yes. As a foreign partner or director you need an NIE (foreigner identification number). We explain the steps.
