faq:
  - q: Do I have to charge VAT on my invoices?
    a: Almost always. Spain has no turnover threshold below which VAT does not apply; only some activities are exempt, such as healthcare or education. We confirm it in the consultation.
  - q: How often is VAT filed?
    a: Every quarter, with form 303, from the 1st to the 20th of the following month; the fourth quarter by 30 January. Companies with more than 6 million euros in annual turnover file every month.
  - q: What happens if I file late?
    a: If you file late without being asked, you pay a 1 % surcharge plus 1 % for each full month of delay; after 12 months, 15 % plus interest. That is why we work ahead of the deadline.
  - q: I have received a notice from the Tax Agency. What should I do?
    a: Do not reply yet; send it to us. We check what they are asking for and reply on time.
