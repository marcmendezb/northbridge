faq:
  - q: GmbH or UG?
    a: A GmbH needs €25,000 of share capital, with at least €12,500 paid in on formation. A UG starts from €1, but must put a quarter of its annual surplus into a reserve. We show you what fits your plans.
  - q: What does it cost on top of your fee?
    a: Notary and commercial register charge official fees, which depend mainly on the share capital. We get the notary's quote and go through it with you.
  - q: How long does it take?
    a: It depends mainly on the register court. With complete documents, we usually plan a few weeks until registration.
  - q: Can I set up a company if I do not live in Germany?
    a: Yes, shareholders can live abroad. We explain what the notary and the bank require in your case.
