faq:
  - q: What is a cap table?
    a: A table showing who owns what percentage of the company and how it changes with each round.
  - q: How do I give my team equity?
    a: With real or virtual shares. If employees receive shares at a discount, taxation can be deferred under section 19a of the German Income Tax Act. We check with you what fits.
  - q: Do you prepare the shareholders' agreement?
    a: 'We prepare it with your lawyer: we provide the figures and their implications; your lawyer drafts the legal text.'
  - q: When should I hire this service?
    a: Before talking to investors. Arriving with your figures in order changes the conversation.
