summary: We file your VAT and taxes with the German tax office (Finanzamt) on time, and remind you before every deadline.
includes:
  - VAT advance returns (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung), monthly or quarterly, and the annual VAT return.

  - Corporate tax and trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) returns for your GmbH.
  - Income tax return for the self-employed, with the income-surplus statement.
  - Deadline calendar with reminders, including prepayments.
  - Replies to tax office queries and assessments.
forWhom:
  - Self-employed people and companies that invoice with VAT.
  - Founders who want to know whether they can stay in the small-business scheme.
  - Startups with clients or suppliers in other EU countries that want to avoid mistakes.
process:
  - title: We review your situation.
    text: We check how often you must file VAT and which deadlines apply.
  - title: We give you your calendar.
    text: Every specific date, with a reminder from us 10 days before.
  - title: We prepare and show you the return.
    text: You see the amount before we submit it.
  - title: We submit and archive.
    text: We confirm the submission via ELSTER and keep the receipt.
faq:
  - q: Do I have to charge VAT?
    a: Yes, unless you qualify as a small business (Kleinunternehmer), meaning your turnover was at most €25,000 in the previous year and does not exceed €100,000 in the current year. We check with you whether it is worth it.
  - q: How often is VAT filed?
    a: Every quarter, by the 10th day after it ends, if the previous year's VAT was between €2,000 and €9,000; above that, every month. A permanent extension (Dauerfristverlängerung) moves the deadline back.
  - q: What happens if I file late?
    a: The tax office can charge a late-filing surcharge. For annual returns it is 0.25% of the tax per month started, at least €25. That is why we work ahead of the deadline.
  - q: I have received a letter from the tax office. What should I do?
    a: Do not reply yet; send it to us. We check what they are asking for and reply on time.
