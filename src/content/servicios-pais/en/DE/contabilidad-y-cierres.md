process:
  - title: We get to know your business.
    text: In 20 minutes we look at your activity, your bank and how you invoice.
  - title: We connect everything.
    text: You give us access to your receipts and bank transactions.
  - title: We record and reconcile every month.
    text: If something does not add up, we tell you and fix it that week.
  - title: We close the year.
    text: We prepare the annual accounts and explain every figure.
faq:
  - q: What do I need to get started?
    a: Your receipts, read access to your bank and the latest annual accounts, if you have them. That is enough to start.
  - q: Do you work with my software?
    a: We work with the usual tools in Germany, such as DATEV, Lexware or sevDesk. If you do not have one yet, we suggest the one that fits best.
  - q: When will I have my annual accounts?
    a: A small GmbH prepares them within six months of its year end and files them with the Company Register within twelve months at the latest. We plan both dates with you.
  - q: My bookkeeping is behind. Can you help?
    a: Yes. We start by bringing it up to date and give you a plan with dates. It is quoted separately, at a fixed price.
