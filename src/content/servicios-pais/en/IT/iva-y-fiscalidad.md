faq:
  - q: 'Do I have to charge VAT on my invoices?'
    a: 'Yes, unless you are a freelancer under the flat-rate scheme (forfettario): income of up to €85,000 in the previous year. If you exceed €100,000 during the year, you leave the scheme and charge VAT from that point. E-invoicing is mandatory for everyone anyway.'
  - q: 'How often is VAT settled?'
    a: 'Every month, by the 16th of the following month. With turnover of up to €500,000 in services or €800,000 in other activities, you can opt to settle quarterly, with 1% interest.'
  - q: 'What happens if I pay late?'
    a: 'Penalties and interest apply. If you correct it yourself before being asked (ravvedimento operoso), the penalty is reduced. That is why we pay ahead of the deadline.'
