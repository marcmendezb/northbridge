faq:
  - q: 'Do I have to charge VAT on my invoices?'
    a: 'Yes, unless you are under the exemption threshold: in 2026, if you invoiced at most €85,000 in sales or €37,500 in services in the previous year. If you exceed €93,500 or €41,250 in the current year, you charge VAT from that point.'
  - q: 'How often is VAT filed?'
    a: 'Under the standard scheme, every month, between the 15th and 24th depending on your company. From 1 January 2027 the simplified scheme ends: up to €1M turnover, the return becomes quarterly.'
  - q: 'What happens if I file late?'
    a: 'Late interest of 0.20% per month applies and, if the return is late, a 10% surcharge (articles 1727 and 1728 of the CGI). That is why we file ahead of the deadline.'
  - q: 'I received a letter from the administration. What should I do?'
