A GmbH or self-employed person in Germany whose financial year is the calendar year has fixed dates: the advance VAT return, the prepayments, wage tax and social security. On top come the annual returns in summer and the publication of the accounts. Here is the whole year and what we need from you before each date.

## VAT: by the 10th

The advance VAT return (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung) is filed and paid **by the 10th day after the end of the period**:

- **Quarterly**, if the previous year's VAT was between €2,000 and €9,000.
- **Monthly**, if it exceeded €9,000.
- If it was €2,000 or less, the tax office can release you from advance returns.

With a **permanent extension (Dauerfristverlängerung)** the deadline moves later. Small businesses (turnover of at most €25,000 in the previous year and at most €100,000 in the current year) do not charge VAT.

## Prepayments

- **Income or corporate tax:** 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December, in the amount the tax office sets.
- **Trade tax (Gewerbesteuer):** 15 February, 15 May, 15 August and 15 November, to the municipality.

## Payroll: wage tax and social security

- **Wage tax return (Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung):** by the 10th day after the period. Monthly if the previous year's wage tax exceeded €5,000; quarterly between €1,080 and €5,000; annually below that.
- **Social security contributions:** on the third-to-last banking day of the month worked.
- **Wage tax certificates (Lohnsteuerbescheinigungen):** by the end of February, to the tax office.

## Annual returns and accounts

- **Previous year's returns** (VAT, income or corporate tax, trade tax): by **31 July**. With a tax adviser the deadline is longer, until the end of February of the second following year.
- **Annual accounts:** a small GmbH prepares them within six months and publishes them in the company register no later than twelve months after the year end.

Filing late can mean a late filing surcharge: for annual returns, 0.25% of the tax per month or part month, at least €25.

## What we need from you

The rule is simple: **each period's documents by the 3rd of the following month.** Issued and received invoices, bank statements and new contracts. With that we file VAT and wage tax on time and your numbers stay current.

If you work with us, you do not have to remember any of these dates: we write to you before each one with what we need and by when.
