An SRL or self-employed person in Italy whose financial year is the calendar year has fixed dates: the F24 on the 16th every month, quarterly VAT, the tax balance in June and the annual returns in October. Here is the whole year and what we need from you before each date.

<Note>
  If a deadline falls on a Saturday or public holiday, it moves to the next working day (art. 7 DL
  70/2011). That is why in 2026 Q1 VAT is due on 18 May and the annual returns on 2 November. We
  remind you before each date with the exact day.
</Note>

## VAT: monthly or quarterly

- **Monthly:** settlement and F24 payment by the **16th of the following month**.
- **Quarterly by option**, if your turnover does not exceed €500,000 in services or €800,000 in other activities: **16 May, 20 August and 16 November**, with 1% interest. Q4 is paid with the annual balance on **16 March**.
- **LIPE report:** the last day of the second month after each quarter (Q2: 30 September).
- **Annual VAT return:** from 1 February to **30 April**.

## Income taxes and IRAP

- **Balance and first advance (acconto):** the last day of the sixth month after year end, **30 June**, or within the following 30 days plus 0.40%.
- **Second advance:** **30 November**.
- **Redditi and IRAP returns:** the last day of the tenth month after year end, **31 October**.

## Payroll

- **Monthly F24:** tax withheld and INPS contributions by the **16th** of the following month.
- **Certificazione Unica:** to the Agency and each employee by **16 March**.
- **Form 770:** by **31 October**.

## What we need from you

The rule is simple: **each period’s documents by the 3rd of the following month.** Issued and received invoices, bank statements and new contracts. With that we pay the F24 on time and your numbers stay current.

If you work with us, you do not have to remember any of these dates: we write to you before each one with what we need and by when.
