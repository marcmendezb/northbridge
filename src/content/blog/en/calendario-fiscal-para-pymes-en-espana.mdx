A company or self-employed person in Spain whose financial year matches the calendar year has four appointments with the Tax Agency every quarter: VAT, withholdings and, depending on the case, instalments. On top of that come the income tax return in June, corporate tax in July and social security every month. Here is the whole year and what we need from you before each date.

<Note>
  If the last day of a deadline is not a working day (Saturday, Sunday or public holiday), it moves
  to the next working day. That is why, in 2026, the withholding summary is due on 2 February and
  corporate tax on 27 July. We remind you before each date with the exact day.
</Note>

## VAT: every quarter, by the 20th

VAT is filed with **form 303**:

- Q1, from 1 to 20 April.
- Q2, from 1 to 20 July.
- Q3, from 1 to 20 October.
- Q4, from 1 to 30 January, together with the **annual summary (form 390)**.

If you pay by direct debit, the deadline ends a few days earlier. Businesses whose turnover exceeded €6,010,121.04 the previous year file every month.

<Note>
  Spain has no turnover threshold below which VAT does not apply. If you start a business, you will
  normally charge VAT from your first invoice.
</Note>

## Instalments: payments on account of the year's tax

- **Companies (form 202):** from 1 to 20 April, October and December.
- **Self-employed under direct assessment (form 130):** every quarter, on the same dates as VAT; the last one by 30 January.

They are advances on the tax you will pay when the year closes. We calculate them from your books.

## Withholdings: every quarter and a summary in January

If you pay salaries, invoices from professionals or rent, you withhold part of it and pay it in yourself:

- **Forms 111 and 115:** from 1 to 20 April, July and October, and from 1 to 20 January for the last quarter.
- **Annual summaries (forms 190 and 180):** by 31 January.

## Income tax and corporate tax: June and July

- **Income tax (form 100):** by 30 June. If you are self-employed, the result of your business goes here.
- **Corporate tax (form 200):** within the 25 calendar days following six months after the year end; with a calendar-year financial year, by 25 July.

Before that, the annual accounts: the directors prepare them within three months of the year end, the shareholders approve them within the first six months and they are filed at the Commercial Registry within the month after approval.

## Transactions with third parties: February

With **form 347** you report, during February, the clients and suppliers with whom your transactions exceeded €3,005.06 in the year, VAT included. It comes from the books, with no extra work for you.

## Social security: every month

Your employees' contributions are paid **within the month following** the one they relate to. We calculate them with the payroll.

## What we need from you

The rule is simple: **each quarter's documents, before the 5th of the following month.** Issued and received invoices, bank statements and any new contract. With that we file VAT and withholdings on time and your figures are up to date.

If you work with us, you do not have to remember any of these dates: we write to you before each one with what we need and by when.
