faq:
  - q: GmbH or AG?
    a: A GmbH is simpler and cheaper to maintain, and suits most SMEs. An AG requires more capital and is usually chosen when there are several shareholders or investment is planned.
  - q: How much capital do I need?
    a: For a GmbH, CHF 20,000, fully paid in. For an AG, CHF 100,000, with at least CHF 50,000 paid in.
  - q: How long does it take?
    a: Between one and three weeks from the moment we have all the documents.
  - q: Can I set up a company if I don’t live in Switzerland?
    a: Yes, but at least one person with signing authority must live in Switzerland. We explain the options.
